Our Inspiration

Saliha Begum (Late)

Saliha Begum’s life was a testament to the truth that wealth is a trust from the Divine. Born into a humble household and married during a time of significant financial hardship, she understood the weight of poverty firsthand. She knew the quiet dignity of those who struggle in silence.

When life transitioned from scarcity to abundance, Saliha did not change her lifestyle—she changed the lives of those around her. Despite her later wealth, she remained a woman of profound simplicity, often preferring simple meals.

Her home became a sanctuary. Throughout the year, she quietly provided monthly stipends to dozens of widows and orphans.

She believed that dignity is as important as the donation itself. We honor her memory not just by building a hospital, but by institutionalizing the compassion she showed to every person who knocked on her door.
